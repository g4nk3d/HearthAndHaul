# Hearth & Haul

A dungeon-diving minigame for World of Warcraft, part of the Nether addon family.

Below the Sleeping Gryphon inn lies the Undercroft. Go down with an empty bag, pick a door on every floor,
fight with your guard and your sword, and fill the bag with finds. The deeper you go, the more they are worth.
When you've had enough, cast your **hearthstone**: ten seconds to get home, while its light draws every
creature nearby. Each hit that gets through knocks the cast back; holding your guard holds it still. Get home
and every coin is banked. Fall, and the bag is lost.

## Features

- Five zones: the Cellars, the Old Catacombs, the Fungal Deep, the Crystal Vault and the endless Rift,
  each with its own creatures, music and loot. Four bosses: Old Thornback, the Gatewarden, Mother Mycel
  and the Faceted King.
- Real-time fighting: strike, watch for the red wind-up, guard at the right moment; stamina and guard breaks.
- Doors with fights, elites, chests (some of them bite), shrines, traps and unknowns.
- A bag to manage: 12 slots, finds of five qualities, swap what you carry for what you find.
- The Sleeping Gryphon between dives: Marga the innkeeper, Dunstan, Lira and Pip the cat; spend banked gold
  on twelve furnishings for the inn.
- Four perks, opened by achievements: Lantern, Thick Cloak, Deep Pockets, Quick Hands.
- Bronze, silver and gold medals for a dive's haul; twelve achievements.
- A daily dive: the same floors for everyone in the world on the same date, with a shared scoreboard (every
  haul is checked by replaying the dive before it counts).
- Your dive is kept after a /reload, to the very moment. Combat pauses the game at once. Nothing runs while
  the window is closed.

## Keys

`F` or the left mouse button: strike. `Space` or the right mouse button: guard. `H`: hearth (again to call it
off). `B`: bag. `E` or `Enter`: open, use, onward. `L`: walk past. `1` `2` `3`: doors. `Esc`: pause.

## Commands

- `/hnh` or `/hearthandhaul`: open or close the game
- `/hnh daily`: today's daily dive
- `/hnh perf`: frame time and memory
- `/hnh debug`: errors and missing art
- `/hnh reset`: forget this character's records (asks first)
